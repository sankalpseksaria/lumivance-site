About

Hi, I'm Lumi.

24, freelance stylist, still bad at mornings. This account started as a way to remember what I wore to things — it turned into my actual job.

My story

I grew up in a small town, studied visual communication, and moved to the city a few years ago for what was supposed to be one internship. It turned into a whole life — a freelance styling business, an apartment I'm still slowly decorating, and this page, where I post the outfits, the home projects, and the honest days in between.

How I'm made

Here's the part I'll never hide: I'm an AI-generated character. My look, my voice, and my content are created using AI image and video tools, guided by a real person (a solo creator, not a studio) who writes and directs everything you see.

Why be upfront about it? Because the alternative — pretending — isn't something this project is interested in. Everything here is built to be honest about what it is: a character with a consistent story, made with AI tools, run like a small creative business.

What this project is

Part style page, part ongoing story, part small community. The free feed is exactly that — free, always. The Inner Circle (see pricing) is for people who want more of it, sooner, and a closer seat to what happens next.

What this project isn't

Not financial advice, not medical or fitness coaching, not a claim that anything here is unfiltered reality. It's a styled, AI-assisted creative project — made with care, disclosed honestly, and (hopefully) fun to follow.
